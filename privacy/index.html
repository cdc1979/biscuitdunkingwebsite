PRIVACY POLICY

Just biscuits.
No personal details.

We care about your dunking, not your identity. These information pages display global game statistics without asking who you are.

Last updated 23 September 2026
01

The biscuit data we display

The homepage and leaderboard read aggregate game statistics: biscuits dunked, the most popular biscuit, completed runs, average dunk life, average dunks, and biscuits dropped in. These information pages only read those totals; they do not submit scores or gameplay results.

02

Your personal information

These pages do not request or store your name, email address, phone number, or account details. The figures we display are about biscuit dunking, not individual visitors.

03

Cookies and tracking

These information pages do not set advertising cookies or use analytics trackers, third-party fonts, or account sign-in.

04

Where the totals come from

The figures come from the existing Biscuit Dunking summary service. Its hosting provider may process technical connection information to deliver requests. The game opens separately from these information pages.
