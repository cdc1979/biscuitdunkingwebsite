THE SHORT VERSION

Privacy, plain
and simple.

We don’t ask who you are. We don’t collect personal details. We keep the biscuit dunking data that makes the game and its global leaderboard work.

LAST UPDATED: 23 SEPTEMBER 2026

What we store

When you play, we store game results: your score, successful dunks, biscuits used, your best dip depth, and when the run was played. To show a score on the public leaderboard, we also attach a randomly generated mug name such as “Mug-A12F”. It isn’t a name you choose, and the game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or account details.

What we use it for

These game records are used to run the game, show your results, and rank scores on the global leaderboard. Leaderboard entries are visible to anyone visiting the site. We do not sell this game data or use it to build advertising profiles.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or account sign-in. Your browser creates a temporary generated mug name while you play; it is not used to identify you between visits.

Where game data is kept

Leaderboard records are stored in Cloudflare Durable Objects, which powers the game’s global scoreboard. The site’s infrastructure provider may process technical connection information as part of delivering the website. Our game database is limited to biscuit-dunking data and generated game labels.

How long it stays

Scores stay on the leaderboard until they are removed or replaced as part of operating the game. The scoreboard shows the ten highest scoring runs.

Questions

If you have a question about the game’s stored score data, please get in touch with the site owner through the channel where you found this game.
